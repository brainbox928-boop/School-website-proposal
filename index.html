About Us

A legacy of learning on the Badagry coast

Founded in 2001, BrainBox Schools has grown from a small nursery into a leading co-educational school, built on discipline, curiosity and service.

🎯 Our Mission

To nurture confident, ethical and capable young people through a rigorous curriculum, dedicated teachers and a caring community.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the school of choice in Lagos State, producing global-minded leaders who give back to Nigeria.

Admissions

Join the BrainBox family

Applications for 2026/2027 are open for Nursery to SSS 2.

How to Apply

  1. Submit the online form. Takes about 5 minutes.
  2. Entrance assessment. A short test and informal interview.
  3. Offer of admission. Results are sent by email within 3 working days.
  4. Accept and pay. Pay the acceptance fee online to secure the place.

Documents Needed

  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Last two report cards
  • 2 passport photographs
  • Immunisation record
